# Stakeholders

| Group | Needs from the portal |
| --- | --- |
| Principal | A safe, manageable Gujarati-first admin area for school configuration, publishing, uploads, results, and account settings. |
| Students, Standard 1–8 | Simple phone-friendly Gujarati access to materials, notices, timetables, downloads, and their own result. |
| Parents | Clear school information and notices, easy resource access, and private individual result lookup. |
| Teachers and public visitors | Reliable public school information, notices, gallery, and shared resources without accounts. |
| School administration | A reusable school-owned portal with controlled publishing, privacy, and recoverable data. |
| Maintainer/developer | Clear architecture, scoped requirements, operational guidance, and straightforward deployment. |
