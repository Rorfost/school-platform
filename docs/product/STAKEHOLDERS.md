# Stakeholders — Rorfost School Platform

| Group | Needs and Expectations |
|---|---|
| **School Principals** | A safe, manageable Gujarati-first admin panel to manage school settings, publish notices/photos, upload marks, and maintain academic structure for their specific school without exposure to or interference from other schools. |
| **Students (Std 1–8)** | Simple, mobile-friendly Gujarati access to study materials, circulars, and private marksheet lookup with single-page print optimization. |
| **Parents & Guardians** | Reliable school information, notices, official contacts, and secure individual examination marksheet access. |
| **Platform Operators** | Predictable, repeatable onboarding runbooks for new schools, cryptographic gateway token management, feature flag toggles, and zero-downtime maintenance procedures. |
| **Teachers & Staff** | Convenient access to school circulars, timetables, and client-side educational calculators (School Tools). |
| **Platform Maintainers** | A modular monolith codebase with clear package boundaries, testable tenant isolation, append-only migrations, and comprehensive architectural documentation. |
